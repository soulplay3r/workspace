More than just a platform, Synergice is a dynamic B2B ecosystem, a reflection of our belief that collaboration transforms potential into progress.

Who we are

Strides Pharma Science develops and manufactures finished dosage forms for regulated and emerging markets. Its manufacturing network holds approvals from authorities including the US FDA, the UK MHRA, Australia's TGA and the WHO, and its products reach patients in more than 100 countries.

In November 2024 Strides consolidated its business-to-business activities under Synergice, with its European hub at Strides Pharma International AG in Zug, Switzerland. Synergice is the single front door to the products Strides makes available to partners.

What partners get

  • Advanced manufacturing capabilities

    Finished dosage forms made across the Strides network, including oral solids, soft gelatin capsules, liquids and sterile products, at sites approved by regulators such as the US FDA, UK MHRA, TGA and WHO.

  • Robust regulatory expertise

    Dossiers compiled to CTD, eCTD or ACTD, registration support in the destination market and inspection-ready documentation shared with partners during evaluation.

  • Innovative pharmaceutical solutions

    Value-added products, lyophilised orally dissolving tablets, women's health hormones and end-to-end biopharmaceutical CDMO services alongside the generics portfolio.

How we approach partnering

We keep the first step simple. Partners should not have to read through specification sheets and supply terms to find out whether a molecule is relevant. Each molecule page answers the first questions in plain language, and the enquiry form asks only for what the partnering team needs to reply.

Detailed documentation, such as dossier status, registration position, stability and bioequivalence data and site inspection history, is shared directly with partners during evaluation, in the format their regulator requires.

Enquiries are handled by the Strides partnering team, so there is no intermediary between a partner and the manufacturing organisation.

Frequently asked questions

Is Synergice part of Strides Pharma Science?

Yes. Synergice is the B2B partnering platform of Strides Pharma Science Limited, and enquiries are handled by the Strides partnering team.

Where is Synergice based?

Strides is headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Its European B2B hub is Strides Pharma International AG in Zug, Switzerland.

Does Synergice sell to patients or pharmacies?

No. Synergice works with pharmaceutical businesses, such as licensing partners, marketing authorisation holders, distributors and institutional buyers.

Which regulators have approved Strides sites?

Strides' manufacturing network holds approvals from authorities including the US FDA, UK MHRA, Australia's TGA, EU GMP inspectorates and the WHO. Which approvals apply to a given product depends on the site that makes it.
