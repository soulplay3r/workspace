    Who we work with

    Synergice is the B2B partnering platform of Strides Pharma Science Limited, a pharmaceutical company founded in 1990 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Strides manufactures finished dosage forms in sites approved by regulators including the US FDA, the UK MHRA, Australia's TGA and the WHO, and supplies more than 100 countries.     Frequently asked questions

    What is Synergice?

    Synergice is the B2B partnering platform of Strides Pharma Science Limited. It brings Strides' partner-ready portfolio together in one place so that licensing partners, marketing authorisation holders and distributors can find molecules and contact the partnering team directly.

    Who can send an enquiry?

    Pharmaceutical companies, distributors, licensing partners and institutional buyers. The site is intended for business partners and is not directed at patients or the general public.

    What information is on each molecule page?

    An answer-first summary, a key facts table (class, ATC code, reference product, first approval, route and dosage form), the approval history, the mechanism of action, approved uses, pharmacokinetics at a glance and frequently asked questions.

    Can I ask about a molecule that is not listed?

    Yes. Use the contact form and name the molecule, dosage form and market. The partnering team will tell you whether it is available within the Strides portfolio or through in-licensing.

    Does Synergice give medical advice?

    No. Molecule pages summarise public prescribing information for business partners. Patients should speak to a healthcare professional.

    Have a molecule in mind?

    Send one short enquiry. The Synergice partnering team at Strides replies directly.
