Frequently asked questions

How are products organised on Synergice?

Products are organised into 12 therapy areas, from cardiovascular and oncology to women's health. Each therapy area lists its molecules, and each molecule has its own page with key facts, approval history and mechanism of action.

What does each molecule page include?

An answer-first summary, a key facts table, approval history, mechanism of action, approved uses, pharmacokinetics at a glance, frequently asked questions and an enquiry form.

Can I enquire about a product that is not on this list?

Yes. The Strides partner portfolio includes more than 250 formulations. Use the contact form and name the molecule, dosage form and market.
