About Ergocalciferol

Ergocalciferol is vitamin D2, a fat-soluble prohormone made by ultraviolet irradiation of ergosterol from yeast and fungi. The liver and kidney convert it into the active hormone, which increases absorption of calcium and phosphate from the gut and supports normal bone mineralisation. High-strength soft capsules are used for hypoparathyroidism, refractory rickets and familial hypophosphataemia, and widely to correct vitamin D deficiency under clinical supervision.

Clinical information

Mechanism of action

Ergocalciferol is hydroxylated in the liver to 25-hydroxyvitamin D2 and in the kidney to 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D2. The active hormone binds the nuclear vitamin D receptor and increases intestinal calcium and phosphate absorption and renal calcium reabsorption.

Indication

Ergocalciferol is indicated for hypoparathyroidism, refractory rickets (vitamin D-resistant rickets) and familial hypophosphataemia. High-strength capsules are also used to treat vitamin D deficiency under clinical supervision, according to local labelling and practice.
