About Anastrozole

Anastrozole is an oral non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor used to treat hormone receptor-positive breast cancer in postmenopausal women. By blocking the aromatase enzyme in fat, muscle and breast tissue, it lowers circulating estrogen by around 80 percent and deprives estrogen-dependent tumour cells of their growth signal. It is taken once daily as adjuvant therapy for early breast cancer and in advanced disease. It was first approved by the US FDA in December 1995.

Clinical information

Mechanism of action

Anastrozole is a triazole that competitively and reversibly binds the haem iron of aromatase (CYP19A1), blocking aromatisation of androstenedione and testosterone to estrone and estradiol. At 1 mg daily it reduces estradiol by about 80 percent and does not meaningfully affect cortisol or aldosterone synthesis.

Indication

Anastrozole is indicated in postmenopausal women for adjuvant treatment of hormone receptor-positive early breast cancer, first-line treatment of hormone receptor-positive or unknown advanced breast cancer, and advanced breast cancer that has progressed after tamoxifen therapy.
