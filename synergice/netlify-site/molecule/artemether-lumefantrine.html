About Artemether / Lumefantrine

Artemether-lumefantrine is an artemisinin-based combination therapy for malaria. It pairs artemether, a fast-acting derivative of artemisinin that rapidly reduces the number of parasites, with lumefantrine, a long-acting partner drug that clears the parasites that remain. Combining two drugs protects each against resistance. It is the most widely used treatment for uncomplicated falciparum malaria worldwide. It was first registered in 1999 and approved by the US FDA in April 2009.

Clinical information

Mechanism of action

Artemether and its metabolite dihydroartemisinin are activated by haem iron inside the parasite, generating free radicals that damage parasite proteins. Lumefantrine is thought to block detoxification of haem into haemozoin, so toxic haem accumulates in the parasite.

Indication

Artemether-lumefantrine is indicated for the treatment of acute, uncomplicated malaria caused by Plasmodium falciparum, including mixed infections, in adults and children weighing at least 5 kg. It is not indicated for prevention of malaria or for severe malaria.
