About Potassium Chloride

Potassium chloride is an electrolyte replenisher used to treat and prevent low blood potassium, a common effect of diuretics and gastrointestinal losses. Low potassium causes muscle weakness and can trigger dangerous heart rhythm disturbances. Oral extended-release forms release potassium slowly to avoid irritation of the gut, and liquid and powder forms suit patients who cannot swallow tablets. The chloride salt also corrects the chloride loss that often accompanies hypokalaemia.

Synergice manufactures potassium Chloride as extended-release capsules and tablets, powder for oral solution, oral solution. Clinical information

Mechanism of action

Potassium is the main intracellular cation and sets the resting membrane potential of nerve and muscle cells. Potassium chloride restores body potassium stores, and the chloride component allows the kidney to correct hypochloraemic metabolic alkalosis, which otherwise sustains renal potassium loss.

Indication

Potassium chloride is indicated for the treatment and prevention of hypokalaemia, with or without metabolic alkalosis, including in digitalis intoxication and hypokalaemic familial periodic paralysis, and in patients at risk of potassium depletion such as those taking diuretics.
