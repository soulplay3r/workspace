Contact the partnering team

In brief

Send an enquiry about any molecule in the Strides portfolio, or about one you do not see listed. Tell us your name, email, how you heard about us and what you want to know. Your enquiry goes directly to the Synergice partnering team at Strides Pharma Science.

What to include

  • The molecule, dosage form and strengths you are interested in
  • The markets you want to supply or register in
  • Whether you already hold a marketing authorisation
  • What you need first: product information, dossier status, samples or commercial terms

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in my enquiry?

Name the molecule, dosage form and market, and say whether you hold an authorisation. The more specific the question, the faster the partnering team can give a useful answer.

Can I enquire about more than one molecule?

Yes. List them in the message field, or choose the main molecule from the list and mention the others in your message.

Who will reply to my enquiry?

A member of the Synergice partnering team at Strides Pharma Science. There is no third-party distributor or broker in between.

Do you share pricing on the website?

No. Commercial terms depend on the market, volume and partnership model, and are discussed directly with the partnering team.
