About this therapy area

Oral corticosteroids are among the most versatile medicines in use. They treat inflammatory, autoimmune, allergic and some malignant diseases, and they replace hormones in adrenal insufficiency. Because doses range from a few milligrams to high-dose pulses, a full range of strengths matters to prescribers and pharmacies.

Prednisone is the reference oral glucocorticoid in many markets. Strides received US FDA approval for prednisone tablets in 2.5 mg and 5 mg strengths.

Molecule summaries

Prednisone

Prednisone is a synthetic glucocorticoid prodrug that the liver converts to prednisolone. Approved by the US FDA in 1955, it is used across medicine to suppress inflammation and immune activity, from asthma and rheumatoid arthritis to transplant rejection and some blood cancers.

Frequently asked questions

Which corticosteroids can I enquire about?

Prednisone is listed. Name any other corticosteroid or endocrine molecule in your enquiry and the partnering team will confirm what is available.

What is the difference between prednisone and prednisolone?

Prednisone is a prodrug that the liver converts to prednisolone, the active steroid. They are used for the same conditions, and different markets prefer one or the other.
