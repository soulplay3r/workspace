About this therapy area

Women's health hormones are a specialist area. Partners need manufacturing segregation for hormones, stable formulations of poorly soluble steroids, and a regulatory file that addresses the safety profile regulators expect for long-term use.

Synergice lists women's health hormones as one of its core portfolio categories. Micronised progesterone in an oil-filled soft capsule is the example shown here: it is used to protect the endometrium during estrogen therapy and to treat secondary amenorrhoea.

Molecule summaries

Progesterone

Progesterone is the natural hormone of the corpus luteum. Oral micronised progesterone, approved by the US FDA in May 1998 as Prometrium, protects the endometrium from hyperplasia in postmenopausal women taking estrogen and treats secondary amenorrhoea. Micronising the hormone and suspending it in oil makes oral dosing practical.

Frequently asked questions

Which women's health products can I ask about?

Progesterone is listed. Synergice's women's health category covers further hormone products, so name the molecule and market in your enquiry.

Why is oral progesterone micronised?

Natural progesterone is poorly absorbed. Reducing the particle size and suspending it in oil increases the amount absorbed and makes oral dosing practical.
