About this therapy area

Pain and inflammation products span prescription and over-the-counter markets. Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) are among the most used medicines in the world, and the dosage form often decides commercial success: liquid-filled softgel capsules, for example, are valued by consumers for fast onset and easy swallowing.

Strides is one of the larger softgel manufacturers supplying regulated markets. This section covers naproxen sodium and ibuprofen, both propionic acid NSAIDs, and colchicine, an alkaloid used for gout flares and familial Mediterranean fever that works through a completely different, anti-inflammatory mechanism.

Molecule summaries

Colchicine

Colchicine is an alkaloid from the autumn crocus that binds tubulin and dampens neutrophil-driven inflammation. Used for gout for centuries, it received its first full US FDA approval as a single-ingredient tablet (Colcrys) in July 2009. It treats and prevents gout flares and is used in familial Mediterranean fever.

Ibuprofen

Ibuprofen is a short-acting non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug that relieves pain, fever and inflammation by inhibiting COX-1 and COX-2. Discovered by Boots in the 1960s, it was launched in the UK in 1969, approved in the US in 1974 and became available over the counter in 1984. It is on the WHO Model List of Essential Medicines.

Naproxen Sodium

Naproxen sodium is a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) that relieves pain, fever and inflammation by inhibiting COX-1 and COX-2 enzymes. Naproxen was first approved by the US FDA in 1976, and naproxen sodium 220 mg became available over the counter in 1994. Its 12- to 17-hour half-life allows dosing every 8 to 12 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Does Synergice offer OTC pain products?

Yes. Strides received US FDA approval for naproxen sodium 220 mg OTC softgel capsules in 2022, and the portfolio includes other NSAIDs. Ask the partnering team about OTC and prescription presentations for your market.

Is colchicine an NSAID?

No. Colchicine is a plant alkaloid that binds tubulin and dampens neutrophil-driven inflammation. It is used for gout flares and familial Mediterranean fever, and it has a narrow therapeutic index with important drug interactions.
